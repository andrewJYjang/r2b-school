R2B Circle

A small circle for coffee, ideas, and real talk.

Unlike most language exchange meetups that stay on the surface, R2B Circle is designed to go somewhere — gently, naturally, over time.

We start light. Introductions. Coffee. Where are you from? Then, if the moment feels right, we go deeper. What are you building? What are you searching for? Why are you in Seoul?

No pressure. No agenda. Just curious people in a room, letting the conversation find its own direction.

20 min
Activity & Coffee Book share, culture talk, or just arriving together. BTS, K-drama, travel stories — anything goes.
30 min
R2B Circle Intro 3 sentences: your name, why you're in Korea, what you're curious about right now.
30 min

Connection Exchange contacts. Plan the next meetup. Sometimes a project or friendship starts here.
How We Introduce Ourselves
1

Name & where
you're from

Simple. We come from everywhere — India, Spain, Nigeria, Japan, the US. Each name carries a whole world with it.

"Hi, I'm Priya from Mumbai. I'm a postdoc researcher at Korea University."
2

Why you're
in Korea

Research, language, work, love, curiosity — every reason is interesting. This is where the real story starts.

"I came for a one-year fellowship, and somehow I've been here three years."
3

What you're
curious about

Not your job title. Not your credentials. What actually lights up your mind these days? AI, art, food, startups, faith?

"I've been obsessed with humanoid robots and what they mean for healthcare."

just talk

No moderator. No slides. Coffee in hand, we follow the curiosity wherever it leads. This is the R2B Circle.

On Arbor Day,
we plant the first
seeds.

On Korea's Arbor Day — the day the country plants trees — R2B School Cohort 1 begins. Symbolic? Yes. Intentional? Absolutely.

A 3-month small-group program for international students, researchers, and builders in Seoul. We read books, discuss AI and careers, share life stories, and help each other find direction in the age of intelligent machines.

Cohort 1 is small — by design. 5 to 10 people. Real conversations. Free to join.
