Philosophy

People are like trees.
We grow stronger
when roots connect.

Metasequoia trees — tall, enduring, ancient — thrive not alone but as a forest. Their roots intertwine underground, sharing nutrients and stability across the whole grove.

R2B International Community was built on this same idea. People come and go. But the connections stay. A passing traveler, a visiting researcher, a friend you met over coffee — these roots remain long after you leave Seoul.

What It's Like

A small circle for coffee, ideas, and real talk.

Unlike most language exchange meetups that stay on the surface, R2B is designed to go somewhere — gently, naturally, over time.

We start light. Introductions. Coffee. Where are you from? Then, if the moment feels right, we go deeper. What are you building? What are you searching for? Why are you in Seoul?

R2B is hosted by a church community in Anam. You're welcome whatever you believe — no pressure, no agenda.

20 min
Arriving & coffee Getting settled, saying hello, catching up with whoever came.
30 min
Introductions Three sentences: your name, why you're in Korea, what you're curious about right now.
30 min
Reading & conversation We read together, then talk about what it means — for us, here, now.
10 min
Connection Exchange contacts. Plan what's next. Sometimes a friendship starts here.
How We Introduce Ourselves
1

Name & where
you're from

Simple. We come from everywhere — India, Spain, Nigeria, Japan, the US. Each name carries a whole world with it.

"Hi, I'm Priya from Mumbai. I'm a postdoc researcher at Korea University."
2

Why you're
in Korea

Research, language, work, curiosity — every reason is interesting. This is where the real story starts.

"I came for one exchange semester, and somehow I'm still here."
3

What you're
curious about

Not your job title. Not your credentials. What actually lights up your mind these days? AI, art, food, startups, faith?

"I've been obsessed with humanoid robots and what they mean for healthcare."
→

Then we
just talk

No moderator. No slides. Coffee in hand, we follow the curiosity wherever it leads.

"What do you want to learn or build in your life?"
